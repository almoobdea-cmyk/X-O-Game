# research/notes.md — cuckoo brood parasitism (peer-reviewed)
- f1 Cuckoos usually remove one host egg before laying their own. Wang et al. 2026, Behavioural Processes; Wang et al. 2023, Ecology and Evolution (DOI via consensus.app links).
- f2 Cuckoo eggs are laid before host incubation starts so the cuckoo chick hatches first; calculated incubation ~1 day shorter than host eggs. Geltsch et al. 2016, Bird Study; Narushin et al. 2024, Int J Parasitol Parasites Wildl.
- f3 The hatchling evicts host eggs/chicks to monopolize parental care; eviction costs ~20-30% growth during the eviction period. Wang et al. 2020, Proc R Soc B; Grim et al. 2009, Behavioral Ecology.
- f4 Hosts keep feeding the cuckoo chick (fosterers feed evictors). Grim et al. 2009, Behavioral Ecology.
