Where the ring went

The real finial ends in a small crescent crest. On the keychain that crest is closed into a full loop, so the eye for the split ring is the top ornament itself and nothing is bolted onto the dome.

  • The loop stands in the same vertical plane as the original crest and takes a standard split ring or a jump ring up to 2.5 mm wire.
  • The finial keeps its eight-lobed bulb and two of its three globes; a 2.8 mm core runs through them into the shell so the pull goes into the dome, not the thin neck.
  • Ribs, arcade arches, niches and window grilles are thickened to printable size. Their counts are unchanged: 64 ribs, 64 arches, 32 niches, 16 panels.
  • The ladder and the roof context of version 1 are left out.

Sized for resin printing or casting. On a filament printer the 0.4 mm details will soften; print with a 0.2 mm nozzle or scale the detail up. The hollow file is open underneath so resin drains.
