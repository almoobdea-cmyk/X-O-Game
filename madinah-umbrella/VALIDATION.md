- Clash checks cover hardware against the mast, the lantern block and the footprint. Part-against-part contact between neighbouring arms is checked only through the casing widths.
- Strut lengths, the lower-strut pin position, the 3.82 m inner arm and the 0.5 m casing stand-off are solved or chosen, not published values.
