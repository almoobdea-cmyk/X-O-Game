How the numbers were derived

No measured survey of the outer dome is published, so every figure here is photogrammetric. The proportions are tight; the absolute scale is the soft part.

  • Profile. The silhouette was traced row by row from a 1500 × 2250 px telephoto image, sampling the green-to-sky edge every 10 px. The shell is not a hemisphere: it rises as a near-straight cone at about 53° from vertical, then turns through a knee into an almost vertical skirt.
  • Counts. Facet edges on the base band give sin(π/N) = 0.19, so N = 16. Each face carries one panel and one pair of niches; four arcade arches and four ribs fit one face, giving 64.
  • Scale. Anchored on the maintenance ladder against the white base (rung pitch about 0.28 m), which puts the cornice at 11.8 m across. Expect ±10 % on absolute sizes, ±3 % on ratios.
  • Cross-check. The green part comes out 8.3 m from cornice to apex; Arabic sources give about 8.9 m for the dome up to the base of the crescent.

Modelled assumptions: the window pyramid is repeated on all four faces (the photographs confirm it on the faces they show), and the calligraphic roundels in the round windows are drawn as geometric rosettes rather than transcribed.
